Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you specialize in?

We focus on Military & Veteran brands, Home & Garden brands, and Islamic & Halal businesses, along with related work for Small & Medium Businesses, E-Commerce brands, and Product Launch campaigns.

How do you find creators for our brand?

We source creators from our network and vet them for audience fit, engagement quality, and alignment with your brand's values — you review and approve every creator before we reach out on your behalf.

How do you measure campaign success?

We don't chase vanity metrics. Every campaign closes with a clear report on reach, engagement, conversions, and ROI, so you know exactly what your investment delivered.

How long does a typical campaign take?

Timelines vary by scope, but most campaigns run 4-8 weeks from strategy call to final report. We'll map out a specific timeline together during your free strategy call.

Do you work with brands outside your three core industries?

Yes — while Military & Veteran, Home & Garden, and Islamic & Halal brands are our specialty, we also work with Small & Medium Businesses, E-Commerce brands, and Product Launch campaigns.
