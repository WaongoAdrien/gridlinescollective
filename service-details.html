Why Sourcing Comes First

The right creator can carry a campaign. The wrong one can quietly sink it — mismatched audience, off-brand tone, engagement that doesn't convert. We treat sourcing as the foundation everything else is built on, not a box to check.

The Right Creator Changes Everything

Most influencer platforms hand you a spreadsheet of follower counts and call it a shortlist. We don't. Every creator we bring to your table has been reviewed by a real person against your brand's specific goals — not a filter for follower count.

  • Audience & fit analysis — we confirm their followers actually look like your customers.
  • Engagement vetting — we look past the vanity numbers for real, active audiences.
  • Brand alignment review — their values, tone, and past content have to hold up to your standards.

This matters most in the spaces we know best. Military & Veteran, Home & Garden, and Islamic & Halal audiences can spot an inauthentic partnership immediately — so we source creators with genuine ties to these communities, not just a willingness to post an ad.

How We Source

  • Discovery: we start from your goals, not a generic creator database, and build a target profile around who actually moves your business forward.
  • Vetting: every candidate is reviewed for audience quality, content history, and brand fit before their name ever reaches you.
  • Shortlist & introduction: you get a curated shortlist with context on why each creator made the cut — you approve before we make first contact.

Once sourcing is done right, every step after it — outreach, content, reporting — gets easier. That's the whole idea.
